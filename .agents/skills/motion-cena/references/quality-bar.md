# Quality bar

The minimum level for every video. The bar measures **what makes a video good**, not a specific look: do not copy any piece. Go through every item before the final render, and again while looking at the contact sheet.

## What makes a video work

1. **One idea, from start to finish.** The central object or scene stays on screen for almost the whole video; everything else is a transformation of it.
2. **The viewer becomes part of the video.** The video can use the person's own attention as proof ("você ficou 7 segundos olhando isso", "you've been looking at this for 7 seconds").
3. **A hook without words.** The first seconds may have no speech; the image holds on its own.
4. **Tension that only resolves at the end.** What is promised at the start happens in the last seconds.
5. **Almost nothing on screen.** One object, one number or one caption at a time; lots of empty space; color accent only at the moment of tension.
6. **A believable object.** The main object has material, reflection and shadow; it looks like a thing, not an icon.
7. **Transitions through the same object.** The scene shrinks into another, a detail becomes the next shot: no cutting to "the next slide".
8. **Short text in two layers.** A small setup line, a large line with the keyword; it enters word by word in time with the speech.
9. **Sound tells the story too.** Silence, tick, long riser, dry impact; a calm, unhurried voice.

## Checklist (fail if anything is missing)

- [ ] The video has **one central idea** you can name in one sentence, and it would not work for another script (`concept.md`)
- [ ] The first 2 seconds hook through the image, not only through the sentence
- [ ] There is at least one **transformation** of the device (the same object becomes something else) instead of a slide change
- [ ] There is a promise or tension at the start that resolves at the end, or an ending that reconnects with the start
- [ ] Low density: each moment has one focus; empty space is intentional
- [ ] Main objects look like things (light, shadow, texture, volume) or have a deliberate graphic style; no loose icon in the center as a crutch
- [ ] The layout uses the whole screen within the safe zone and changes over the course of the video
- [ ] Designed sound: silence, ambience and effects with intent, not just a "pop" on every appearance
- [ ] **It does not look like any delivered video** (`scripts/registro.py --counts`) (device, technique, camera, palette, role of the text)
