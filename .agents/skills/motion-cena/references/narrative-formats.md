# Narrative formats (the story's "packaging")

Same topic → completely different videos. Choose the format before the script; Claude writes much better scripts when it knows which story it is telling.

## Catalog

| Format | Structure | Tension comes from | Good when | Typical visual |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Comparison** | A vs B, side by side, reveals a gap | The distance between the two sides | Data with contrast (leaders × the rest, before × now) | Split screen, diverging bars, balance scale |
| **Transformation** | Before → process → after | Curiosity about the "how" | Process, case study, tool | Wipe/morph from state A to B |
| **Experiment** | "Tentei X, olha o que aconteceu" ("I tried X, look what happened") | Uncertain outcome | Personal test, new tool | Stopwatch, checklist, reveal |
| **Tutorial** | Step 1, 2, 3 | Promise of a skill | Repeatable method | Big numbering, UI mockups |
| **Listicle** | "4 maneiras de…" ("4 ways to…") | Completing the list | Many equivalent options | Item counter, cards |
| **Ranking** | Worst → best | Who wins | Comparable options with a verdict | Podium, tier list, arrows |
| **Myth × truth** | Common belief → data that knocks it down | Broken expectation | Counterintuitive data | "FALSO" stamp ("FALSE"), crossing out text |
| **Forecast / trend** | Where we are → where it is going | The future / fear of being left behind | Time series | Rising line, timeline |
| **Behind the scenes** | How it was made | Exclusive access | Your own process | Stylized screen recording |
| **Character story** | Someone faces a problem → solves it | Empathy | Concrete case | Avatar/icon traveling a journey |
| **Open question** | Strong question → evidence → answer | Wanting the answer | Controversial topic | The question on screen for the whole video |
| **Manifesto** | Short statements that escalate → final line | Rhythm and identification | Motivational, brand, positioning | Kinetic typography, 1 sentence per screen, slam on the peaks |
| **Explanation by analogy** | Question → everyday analogy → real mechanism → example | "Agora entendi" ("Now I get it") | Technical/abstract concept | The analogy's object transforms into the real diagram (match cut) |

## How to evaluate (do it explicitly in plan.md)

For each candidate (min. 4), fill in:

```
### <Formato>
Ângulo: <uma frase — a história específica>
Tensão: <o que faz a pessoa querer ver o fim>
Melhores takes da fonte que sustentam: <2–3, com página>
Risco: <o que pode deixar fraco>
Nota (1–5): retenção _ / clareza _ / fit com público _ / potencial visual _
```

Pick the highest total; on a tie → the highest **visual potential** (this is motion design). Justify it in 2–3 sentences.

## Example (a16z report on AI)

- Listicle "5 dados do relatório" ("5 figures from the report") → informative, zero tension. Low score.
- **Comparison**: "quase todas as grandes empresas dizem ter plano de IA; só uma fração mede resultado. Adoção larga, mas rasa, piscina de um metro. Um grupo pequeno nada no fundo e gasta muitas vezes mais por funcionário, e a distância cresce." (almost every large company says it has an AI plan; only a fraction measures results. Wide but shallow adoption, a one-meter-deep pool. A small group swims in the deep end and spends many times more per employee, and the gap keeps growing.) → tension + a visual metaphor (the pool). Chosen.
