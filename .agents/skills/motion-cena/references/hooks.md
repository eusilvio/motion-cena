# Hook with a trigger (carousel cover, first 3 seconds of the video)

The hook has one job: make the person **open it and stay to the end**. Informing comes later. A cover that already delivers the conclusion ("a maioria das pessoas passa horas no celular", most people spend hours on their phone) kills the post: the person read it, agreed and scrolled on. They must leave the cover with a question in their head that only the next slides answer.

Examples below are pt-BR; write hooks in the user's language.

## The test (run it on every hook before choosing)

1. **Gap**: reading only the cover, did the person get everything? If yes, it fails. A piece must be missing (which, who, why, how, how much) that they want to know.
2. **Close to home**: does the person see themselves in it (their wallet, home, kid, job, routine)? Data about "os brasileiros" (Brazilians) with no bridge to them is news, not a trigger.
3. **Concrete**: there is a number, object or scene you can see (half the month, the bill, the phone screen). Abstract ("a situação financeira preocupa", the financial situation is worrying) fails.
4. **Truth**: the trigger comes from the fact, with a source. Exaggerating or promising what the post doesn't deliver burns trust, which is what the profile is building.
5. **No AI bait**: no "você não vai acreditar", "o segredo?", "ninguém te conta isso", "isso muda tudo" (`writing-style.md`; pt-BR list, in other languages avoid their equivalents). The trigger comes from a well-chosen fact, not from a punchline.

Score 0 to 2 on each criterion; only a hook with 8+ and gap = 2 goes live.

## Triggers (write 5 candidates, each from a different trigger)

| Trigger | How it works | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Gap** | a number or fact that begs an explanation; the answer is in slide 2–3 | "Tem um hábito que quase todo mundo tem de manhã. E não é o café." |
| **Close to home / reframing** | translates the data into a unit the person feels (days of the month, hours, salary) | "Quem passa 4 horas por dia no celular gasta 2 meses por ano olhando pra tela." |
| **Self-diagnosis** | the person tests themselves while reading | "Abre o tempo de tela do seu celular. Se passa de 4 horas, você está acima da média." |
| **Against the belief** | a fact that contradicts what they think (with data, no symmetric phrase) | "Quase todo mundo que diz não ter tempo passa horas no mesmo app, e não é o do trabalho." |
| **Loss / risk** | what they lose if they don't know | "O atraso médio de quem tá devendo passou de 2 meses. Os juros contam todo dia." |
| **Behind the scenes** | the author shows their own home, number, mistake | "A gente quase entrou nessa estatística. Foi aqui que mudou." |
| **Promise + proof** | a concrete result, and the post shows how it was reached | "Fiz um app em 18 dias sem largar o emprego. Os 6 passos tão aqui." |

Best combination: **close to home + gap** (a big sentence that stings + a small line that opens the question and points to the swipe). E.g. title "Quem passa 4 horas por dia no celular gasta 2 meses por ano olhando pra tela." + line "E o app que mais come esse tempo não é o que você pensa →". (Numbers in the examples are illustrative; in the piece, only sourced data.)

## Carousel: the swipe chain

- **Cover** = hook (title) + a swipe line that opens the gap (instead of the generic "Arrasta pro lado" (swipe sideways), or alongside it).
- **Each slide ends pulling the next one**: the last line anticipates what comes ("E a maior parte dessa dívida mora num lugar só →"). A slide that closes neatly is where the person leaves.
- **Answer the cover's gap by slide 3**, never on the last one: whoever swiped gets paid early, and a new gap opens.
- **The cover visual tells the hook without reading** (the month half painted, the bill, the house with the lights on): it is what stops the thumb before the text.
- **First line of the post caption** = another version of the hook (the feed shows only ~125 characters before "mais", the "more" link).

## Video

Same rules in the first 3 seconds: speech + image already at frame 0. The gap opened in the hook pays off mid-video, and the ending can reopen it (loop).

## Logging it in the plan

In `plan.md` (or the carousel's `brief.md`), a "Hooks" table: the 5 candidates, each one's trigger, the test score and the chosen one. On delivery, say which trigger was used and why.
